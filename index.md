I am a Visiting Assistant Professor at Universidad de los Andes.

I am an applied microeconomist. I study how incentives and market structure determine the effectiveness of public policy. My work combines quasi-experimental methods with equilibrium modeling to understand how schools, firms, and individuals respond to government intervention, with a focus on education and credit markets in Latin America.
